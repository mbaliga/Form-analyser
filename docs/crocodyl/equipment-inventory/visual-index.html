Scope and count notes. This is an initial source-linked directory, not a complete US/EU market scrape. Only the companion riser gallery currently contains product cards; accessory categories link to live retailer catalogues. Product photos, live variants, stock and prices remain on seller or manufacturer pages. The source returns 117 target stabiliser listings and 102 finger-tab listings; both counts include options/variant listings. The focused recurve-backpack and takedown-case category pages do not expose enough indexed individual products to support a truthful claim of 50 distinct models each. The limb category does not expose a reliable indexed total, and it does not provide 300 verified model/length/weight combinations in the material checked here. I have not padded those counts with invented products or assumed options.

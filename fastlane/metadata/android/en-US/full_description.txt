Free, open archery form analysis. Put your phone on a tripod beside you, shoot a
session, and get a per-shot read on your form instead of a general opinion about
your technique.

HOW IT WORKS
Crocodyl watches you through the camera and estimates your pose on device. It
builds a baseline from your own shots, not from a textbook archer, then scores
every later shot as a deviation from that baseline. Your good is the reference.

WHAT IT MEASURES
• Form: bow-arm angle, steadiness through the hold, and the shot sequence from
  set-up through hold to release.
• Stability: a per-shot deviation score against your personal baseline.
• Fatigue: how your form decays across a session, as a trajectory rather than a
  single number.
• The part that matters: it correlates those signals against the score you
  actually shot. A form change that does not move the arrow is not an improvement.

WHY ARCHERY FIRST
Archery is a well-posed problem. Discrete repeatable shots, a known external load
in the draw weight, a quasi-static hold, and the draw arm sitting in the camera's
good plane. Best of all there is a real arrow score to validate against, which is
what stops this being another app that tells you things it cannot check.

ON YOUR DEVICE
• Pose estimation runs locally. Video never leaves your phone and is not recorded
  to a server.
• The pose model is bundled in the app, so there is no first-run download and it
  works with no connection at all.
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Your sessions are stored on your device and are deleted when you uninstall.

NO HARDWARE NEEDED
This app is camera-only. There is nothing to mount on your bow and nothing to buy.

Crocodyl is free and open source.
